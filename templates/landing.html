Privacy, handled thoughtfully

Your reflections stay yours

What you write and track in Serenity is personal, so it's treated that way. Your entries are tied to your account and only used to power the features you choose to use.

  • Entries are associated with your account, not shared publicly or with other users.
  • Reasonable technical and access safeguards help protect your information.
  • You can review, edit, or delete your own entries where supported.
